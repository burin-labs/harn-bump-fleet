- Hosted platform certification now stops early when a lane reaches a terminal
  failure. `parallel settle` still records every lane's outcome for the receipt,
  but a lane that fails publishes its cause to a task-group `shared_cell` and the
  siblings check it between polls instead of running out their 720-poll ceiling.
  An abandoned lane is recorded as `lane_abandoned` and never becomes the
  reported reason, so the receipt still names what actually went red.
