Repinned to Harn v0.9.12 and adopted its first-class primitives, deleting the hand-rolled duplicates the release harnesses previously carried. The removed `std/agent/stack` (`agent_llm_caller`) is replaced by `with_retry(default_llm_caller(), ...)` from `std/llm/handlers` and `agent_sanitize_model_options` now imports from `std/agent/options`; the bump-summary `<think>`-block strip and the release-audit `strip_code_fence_lines` helper are replaced by `strip_think_blocks` / `strip_code_fences` from `std/llm/safe` (the latter was upstreamed from this repo). The planner preflight health-gate is retained because it probes reachability, spend, and option-shape before the real audit — behavior the transport-only `models:` ladder does not subsume — and the validate-to-repair loop is annotated for a later cutover onto the workflow stage retry-with-feedback primitive.
