Release readiness now decides on the union of the target repository's ruleset-required contexts and a typed `release_required_contexts` list declared per repository in `fleet.toml`. Rulesets stay the authority for what may merge; they are not the authority for what may ship, so a lane that runs on every default-branch push but is not ruleset-required is no longer in neither set. A declared context that never reported is a named pending row with a count, not an omission and not a pass; a declared context no workflow at the candidate revision produces refuses the cut by name rather than being silently dropped. A repository that declares nothing keeps exactly the previous behavior.
