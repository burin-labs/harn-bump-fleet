A shipped attempt no longer blocks retiring the orphans beside it. `abandon_release_attempts.harn` stopped on any retained attempt, which is right before a tag exists — freeing a subset leaves recovery wedged — but wrong afterwards: the tag-recovered attempt is terminal and `sweep_release_refs` retires it, so blocking on it stranded the losing candidates that nothing else can ever retire. Only a live claim (open PR, unresolved release, moved or malformed ref) blocks now.
