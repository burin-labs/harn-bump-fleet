A failed release step now records a bounded, credential-redacted excerpt of its stderr and stdout in the run events, and the generated-content checks persist their full output to `live-logs/<step>.log` and lift that log's tail into the failure event. Previously a failed step recorded only `exit_code`, which for `make` is always 2, so a transient failure became unreproducible as soon as its workspace moved on. Successful steps stay quiet.
