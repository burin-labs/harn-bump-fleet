`abandon_release_attempts.harn` now retires the losing candidates a release leaves behind. It compared whether the version had a tag at all, so once any candidate shipped every sibling attempt read as `claimed_by_tag` and was refused — even though no tag recovers them and `sweep_release_refs` retains them forever. A tag now claims only the exact OID it recovers; anything else is reported as `orphaned_by_shipped_tag` and archived.
