Drift in a fleet projection no longer stops the fleet from bumping. `Bump Harn
Fleet` ran `check_fleet_policy.harn` as its first step under `set -euo
pipefail`, so a non-conforming projection aborted the job before a single
repository was dispatched. Because `[policy] bump_workflow` pins a SHA of a file
that moves every few days — and every move puts all 26 fleet-owned adapters out
of conformance at once — that check was red by construction and grounded the
fleet for three days. It now runs after dispatch, so drift still fails the run
and still writes its receipt, but no longer decides whether the fleet may bump.
